Tiny games for tiny breaks.

Pick one up at the bus stop, put it down at your stop. You're playing in seconds, and no ad ever cuts into a round.

Three things we promise.

  • You're playing in seconds

    The first tap is a game tap. No sign-up, no account, no five-minute tutorial before the fun starts.

  • No ads in the middle of a level

    Want an extra life or double coins? Watch a short video, if you feel like it. It's always your call, never a surprise.

  • Skip the in-between ads for good

    One small purchase turns off the ads between levels, forever. The bonus videos stay around in case you still want the reward.
