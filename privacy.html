Privacy Policy

Effective: TODO-DATE · Applies to all Yalla Games apps on Google Play

Yalla Games ("we", "us") is made by dev.Puneet. This policy explains what data our games collect, why, and your choices.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, or account. Our games and the services they use automatically collect:

  • Device and app information: device model, OS version, language, app version.
  • Identifiers: the Android Advertising ID (if you allow it), app-instance IDs, and IP address. The IP address is used to estimate your approximate country or region.
  • Gameplay and usage: levels played, session length, and which features and ads you interact with.
  • Crash and performance logs, so we can fix bugs.
  • Purchase records: Google Play processes payments. We receive only confirmation of what was bought, never your payment details.

Why we use it

  • To run the game, save progress, and restore purchases.
  • To show ads, including rewarded ads you choose to watch, and to measure ad performance.
  • To understand how the game is played so we can improve difficulty, fix bugs, and decide what to build next.

Your choices

  • Consent: where the law requires it (e.g. EEA, UK, and certain US states), we ask for your consent before personalized ads. You can change your choice from the game's settings.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset it or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Remove ads: a one-time purchase turns off interstitial ads. Rewarded ads stay optional.
  • Deletion or access requests: email us at the address below. Since we don't collect names or accounts, we may ask for an in-game ID shown in Settings.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Retention and security

Analytics data is kept only as long as it's useful for improving our games, according to each provider's retention settings. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

Changes

We'll update this page when our practices change and revise the effective date above.
